## Pilot observation (optional; confidential)

Record module, setup route (local/WSL/hosted), prior experience category, setup minutes, active task minutes, download/wait minutes, stall point and recovery/help used. Record which pages were skipped and why; ask rather than infer disengagement. Note interface/accessibility barriers without collecting diagnoses. Record whether the task was completed independently, with help or not completed. Use these observations to revise instructions and workload; do not treat AI execution time as learner time.
