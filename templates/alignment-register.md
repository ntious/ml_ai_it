## Rules

- Mark anything not checked against the authoritative source as Unverified.
- Record a bridge activity whenever the module's data label (Synthetic, Offline fixture) falls short of the outcome's claim.
- Do not record student work, grades or restricted assessment text in the public repository.
- Alignment records planned coverage. It does not show attainment or accreditation compliance.

## Open decisions

List items the course owner must settle, such as whether a live provider call is required, which assessments count as direct evidence, and who confirms institutional approval.
