# Community conduct

Help learners ask questions, disagree about evidence, and improve materials respectfully. Discuss ideas and reproducible behavior. Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted contact, and sharing another person's private information.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions, explain corrective action where appropriate, and restrict repeated disruption. Apply decisions consistently and protect reporters' privacy.

## Reporting

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. A private reporting contact and moderation backup are not yet configured. The owner must establish an accessible private route before open community release. In an adopted course, use established institutional reporting channels.

## Contributions

Preserve attribution and data rights, explain uncertainty, welcome beginner questions, and report barriers without requesting a diagnosis. Read CONTRIBUTING.md and the release checklist.
