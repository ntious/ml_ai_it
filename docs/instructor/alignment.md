## Authority and limits

The original master syllabus, exact original module-outcome register, and earlier private planning attachments are not available for independent rereading in this audit environment. Their exact wording and completeness have not been certified here. A file reference in prior conversation is not a substitute for the source. Do not invent missing Oxx identifiers or attribute paraphrases as official wording.

Modules 4–14 retain their existing original-register references where present. New instructor notes for Modules 1–3 identify repository outcomes and provisional course-domain opportunities without fabricating an original-outcome crosswalk. Confirm each against the authoritative documents before formal assessment or accreditation use.

## Advisory ABET categories

A1 analysis, A2 solution evaluation, A3 communication, and A4 professional/ethical judgment are internal planning shorthand. They are not official ABET outcome numbers, accreditation findings, or evidence of master's-level attainment. Individual work does not establish teamwork or leadership. Use the institution's adopted criteria and assessment process for any formal mapping.

## Complete the formal crosswalk

For each repository outcome, record the exact authoritative original outcome and source location, the course outcome, the assessed artifact, the applicable adopted program criterion, the assessor, and the review date. Mark a relationship as direct evidence, supporting practice, or no mapping. Leave a source-unknown field explicitly unverified until the document owner checks it. No invented official alignment is needed for independent use of these lessons.
