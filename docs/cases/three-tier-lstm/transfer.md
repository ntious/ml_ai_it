# Transfer the workflow to your own project

**Target:** reuse the design discipline with a question and dataset you can justify. Allow about 15 minutes after the integration checkpoint or when planning an existing project. The adopting course's actual project rules still apply; this companion creates no new graded assessment.

Use the reusable [pipeline-passport template](../../../templates/pipeline-passport.md) if you want a separate record.

## Complete a short pipeline passport

| Decision | What to record for your own task |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | A stakeholder, observation, target/output, decision time, and likely error costs |
| Source | Real permitted data, citation/rights, population/period, provenance, and limitations |
| Preparation | Fields, units, missingness/duplicates, IDs, and representation |
| Evaluation boundary | Entity/time split, training-only learned steps, validation selection, and frozen final evaluation |
| Methods | Relevant simple baseline and justified alternatives; optional resampling or neural complexity only for a reason |
| Evidence | Metrics with denominators, uncertainty, error slices, readable tables/figures, and source/run identity |
| Use | Inference contract, replay, ownership, privacy, monitoring, fallback, or a reason not to deploy |
| Communication | A bounded conclusion, limitations, reproducible files/commands, and a decision you can explain independently |

You need not match the research repository file-for-file, use its medical domain, create synthetic patient records, or choose an LSTM. Use the [completed IT capstone](../../../projects/phishing-triage/README.md) when you want a runnable multi-stage exemplar, and [project companion](../../project.md) for the course's four broad milestones.

## Choose optional depth

After the core explanation, predeclare one investigation: compare a simpler model, redesign a validation boundary, check label/feature alignment, or investigate whether a separate generative component has a useful role. Record the expected evidence and the budget. These are proposals until you execute and verify them; do not label them new results.

## Work with or without GenAI

You may ask an assistant to question your diagram or suggest a missing handoff when permitted. Verify its claims against exact files and outputs, keep evaluation boundaries intact, and disclose useful assistance under local rules. The same task can be completed with the lesson, documentation, and public feedback without an assistant or paid account.

## Completion and feedback

Write a one-paragraph workflow explanation, then point to two concrete handoffs and one unresolved limitation. You can give this explanation in a live session or retain readable text independently; screenshots and synchronous attendance are not required by the companion. Compare [transfer feedback](feedback.md#transfer).
