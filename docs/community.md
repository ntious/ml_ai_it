# Ask, share and contribute

## Ask a question

- **Enrolled in a course that uses this resource:** use your instructor's support channel first. Your instructor sets the rules for collaboration and AI use.
- **Studying independently:** open a "Learning question" issue on the [repository](https://github.com/ntious/ml_ai_it/issues) if you have access. Include the module, the command or notebook cell, the full error text, your Python version and what you expected. Never include passwords, personal data or student records.
- **Accessibility barrier:** use the [accessibility reporting guide](accessibility/index.md).

Questions that several learners share become improvements to the lessons, so asking helps the next person too.

## Share strong work

The course may showcase learner projects that show careful evidence: a stated decision, a fair baseline, a preserved final test, and honest limits. A showcase entry needs:

1. Written consent from the learner, using the [exemplar consent template](../templates/exemplar-consent.md).
2. Approval from the learner's instructor when the work was submitted for a course.
3. Removal of names, student IDs, grades and any personal or confidential data.
4. Reproducible commands and source attribution.

No learner work is published without all four. The completed [capstone exemplar](../projects/phishing-triage/README.md) shows the standard of evidence; it was written for the course.

## Contribute

Corrections, clearer explanations and new exercises are welcome. Read [contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [maintain and review](maintainers.md). Every change keeps the module learning outcomes and the evaluation boundaries.
